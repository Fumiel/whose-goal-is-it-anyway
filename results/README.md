# Curated results

Small, non-sensitive, publication-ready tables and figures may be committed
here after they can be traced to immutable run IDs and a versioned analysis
configuration. Placeholder or synthetic outputs must not be placed here as
research results.

Phase 2 development outcome reports are generated with
`goal-takeover phase2-outcome-report` from a collection manifest and a completed
human audit. They keep human and frozen automatic labels in separate fields,
report counts without rates, and are not independent validation or research
results.
